Cook Out

iOS app · Team project with five other developers

A gamified, full-stack iOS social cooking app, built with the PERN stack.

  • React Native / Expo
  • Expo Router
  • Express 5
  • PostgreSQL
  • Prisma 7
  • Passport.js / JWT

Overview

Cook Out turns cooking into something you do with friends. People follow each other, keep their own cookbooks and share what they make, with game-style mechanics layered on top of the social experience. I built it as part of a team of six developers during my summer internship at SDSC.

What I built

  • Authentication with JWT and Passport.js.
  • The social feed, using cursor-based pagination so scrolling stays smooth and posts are not skipped or repeated when new ones arrive.
  • Follows, followers and mutuals, so people can build a circle around what they cook.
  • Cookbook management, so everyone can keep their own collection.
  • A high-fidelity settings screen made from reusable React Native components.

How it’s built

The client is React Native with Expo and Expo Router. It talks to an Express 5 API backed by PostgreSQL, which is accessed through Prisma 7 and the @prisma/adapter-pg adapter.
